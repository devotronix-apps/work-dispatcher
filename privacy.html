Privacy policy

Work Dispatcher, provided by Devotronix, is an iPhone and iPad client for Google Calendar. This policy describes the app and this public support website. Questions and deletion requests can be sent to devotronix@gmail.com.

Google account and calendar information

With your permission, the app accesses your Google account identity and email address, your accessible calendar list, and event information such as titles, times, addresses, notes, guests, and recurrence details. Complete Google event records are retained where needed to preserve event fields when you make changes.

The app requests read-only access to your calendar list and access to view and manage Google Calendar events. It uses these permissions to display schedules and carry out the create, edit, delete, and reassignment actions you request. Read-only calendars cannot be edited. Google Calendar may notify existing guests when you change an event.

Work Dispatcher connects directly to Google. Calendar information is not routed through or stored on a Work Dispatcher-operated server. Changes you save are stored in your Google Calendar account and are subject to Google's retention settings and policies.

Data stored on your device

The app caches your Google account ID and email, calendar and event records, route nicknames and color preferences, and a journal of pending changes. This supports offline access and safe synchronization. Cached periods are retained until you remove local account data or uninstall the app. Google Sign-In manages authentication credentials using its supported iOS storage.

Signing out keeps your cached account data and pending changes on the device so you can resume later. It does not by itself revoke the app's Google authorization.

Addresses and location

Address suggestions and map lookups use Apple Maps. Search text, event addresses you ask to locate, and a regional location bias may be sent to Apple to return results. Location permission is requested only for nearby address suggestions. The app does not track your location in the background. You can deny location access and type an address manually, and change access in iOS Settings.

Third-party services and SDKs

Google Calendar and Google Sign-In process data under Google's privacy policy. Google Sign-In's supplied SDK privacy manifest describes linked account/contact information, account and device identifiers, coarse location, and other data and usage information for authentication, app functionality, and SDK analytics. The precise information Google processes depends on the account, SDK behavior, and services used. Work Dispatcher itself reads your Google account ID/email and authorized calendar records; it does not request your phone number.

Apple Maps and iOS services are subject to Apple's privacy policy. The app does not include advertising or a separate Work Dispatcher analytics service, and does not sell Google Calendar data or use it for advertising or AI-model training.

If you use TestFlight, Apple may collect and share beta-test information, crash reports, and feedback with the developer as described in Apple's TestFlight privacy notice. Support messages and beta feedback are used to respond to requests and improve reliability; contact support to request deletion of feedback held by the developer.

Google API Limited Use

Work Dispatcher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google API data is used for the scheduling and synchronization features you request.

Disconnecting and deleting local data

  • In the app, open the menu, then Privacy and account. Remove local data signs you out and deletes the current account's cached schedules, route preferences, and pending changes from this device.
  • Disconnect Google and remove local data also asks Google to revoke this app's account access. Revocation requires an internet connection. If it fails, the app reports the error so you can retry.
  • You can revoke access separately at Google account connections. Google Calendar events already saved in Google are not deleted by removing the app's local data.
  • Deleting local data permanently discards pending changes and sync checkpoints. Some pending changes may already have been partly applied to Google; review them before deleting. Google retains control of data stored in your Google account.
  • Local caches are scoped to the Google account used in the app. To remove another previously used account's cache, sign in to that account and remove its local data, or uninstall the app to remove its local app container. Device backups may retain data according to your Apple backup settings.

This website

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no contact form, analytics scripts, advertising, or Work Dispatcher tracking cookies. GitHub may process access information such as IP addresses and request logs under the GitHub privacy statement. Email links open your mail app; we receive information only if you choose to send a message.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date will be updated on this page. For privacy questions, support, or requests concerning information you have sent to the developer, email devotronix@gmail.com.
